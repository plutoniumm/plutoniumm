Ray optics

A 2D ray optics playground. Rays follow Snell's law with a wavelength dependent refractive index (Cauchy dispersion), so white light splits in glass. Surface losses follow the Fresnel equations, and steep internal angles give total internal reflection. Add elements from the palette, drag them to move, and drag the round handle to rotate. Click an element to edit it.

Notes

The lens is an ideal thin lens: it obeys 1/v + 1/u = 1/f exactly, with no aberration and no dispersion. Crosses mark its focal points when selected. Glass uses n(λ) = A + B/λ² with B in µm²; B near 0.004 matches crown glass, larger values exaggerate the rainbow. Ray brightness drops at each partial reflection and rays dimmer than 3% are dropped. Bounces are capped, so the trace cannot hang.
