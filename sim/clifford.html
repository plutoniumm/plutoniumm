Clifford simulator

Circuits made of H, S and CNOT can entangle every qubit on the board, yet a classical computer simulates them in polynomial time. Instead of 2n amplitudes you track the 2n Pauli operators that stabilize the state (Aaronson and Gottesman, quant-ph/0406196). Pick a gate, click the grid. The state below updates after every edit.
